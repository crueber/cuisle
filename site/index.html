Standups that move.

Cuisle turns your Jira board into a screen built for the meeting: one person at a time, every ticket they own, and a pulse track that shows how each one moved. No more scrolling a shared board while someone hunts for their work.

Your board is built for planning.
Your standup isn’t.

A sprint board shows columns. A standup is about people: what each of them is carrying, what’s stuck, and what quietly hasn’t moved in a week. Cuisle reshapes the same Jira data around the speaker, and shows the last few weeks of every ticket at a glance.

Built for the fifteen minutes you spend together

Everything on one screen. Nothing hidden to make it fit.

One person at a time

When it’s Iris’s turn, the screen shows all of Iris’s tickets, grouped in the order you walk the board: QA first, then review, in progress and to do. Done comes last. Press ↓ and it’s the next person.

  • Speaking order you control: shuffle daily, sort by most active, or set it yourself
  • Rows tighten before anything scrolls; tickets never collapse into counts

The whole board, at a glance

Everyone’s work side by side, unassigned tickets first so nothing falls through the cracks, then what finished this sprint and the top of the backlog. B flips between the board and the person you were on.

  • Unassigned work called out in red, every time
  • Days-in-lane counters flag what’s been sitting too long

Open any ticket, right there

The description with its images, every comment, all the fields including custom ones, and the development activity. Move it, reassign it, flag it or comment, without leaving standup or opening a browser.

  • Opens instantly and offline: content is synced up front
  • Changes go to Jira only when you make them, once, and roll back if Jira says no

Legible over a screen share

Big monospaced keys, high contrast, light and dark themes, and a Large (TV) text size for the conference room screen. Several teams? Each standup gets a tab, and switching is instant because the data is already on your machine.

  • Follows your system theme, or pick one
  • 1–9 or ← → to switch standups

Set it up once. It saves as you go.

Pick a space and a board and Cuisle maps the columns to lanes for you. Every setting saves the moment you change it; there’s no Save button. Per-lane limits, walk order, pulse length, speakers and exports are all one screen away.

  • Status mapping with per-lane ordering
  • Export a standup as JSON to a folder, by hand or after every sync

Local first

Your data stays on your machine.

⌂

Synced once, kept locally

Tickets and their history live in a database on your computer. Cuisle opens instantly, works offline and builds up history over time. History is never pruned.

⇅

Good to Jira’s API

Cuisle talks to Jira only when you ask. No polling, no telemetry. It fetches only what changed, so a sync with nothing new is a handful of requests whatever the board size.

⚿

Secrets in the keychain

Your API token goes straight into the OS keychain. It never touches the database, logs, exports or crash output.

Get started in two minutes

Install, connect, run standup.

  1. Download the build for your system from GitHub Releases.
  2. Connect with your Jira Cloud site, email and an API token.
  3. Pick a board, press R to sync, and you’re ready for tomorrow’s standup.

macOS

Universal .dmg for Apple Silicon and Intel. It’s self-signed, so the first time, right-click Cuisle.app → Open.

Windows

Installer .exe. If SmartScreen appears, choose More info → Run anyway.

Linux

.AppImage or .deb. Needs a Secret Service keyring such as GNOME Keyring or KWallet.

Every release ships SHA256SUMS.txt so you can verify your download.
